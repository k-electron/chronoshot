# Design: Enemy Catalog, AI Pathing & Skills

## Context

ChronoShot is built around deterministic 60 Hz physics (`FixedStepSimulator`), time dilation driven by player movement (`TimeGovernor`), continuous collision detection ballistics, and procedural Web Audio. Currently, `Enemy.ts` units are stationary turrets that only adjust their `aimAngle` when line-of-sight is unobstructed and are destroyed in a single hit.

See `proposal.md` for motivation and `specs/combat-arena/spec.md` for behavior requirements.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- Deliver a unified, extensible `Enemy` configuration architecture supporting 5 archetypes: Pistol Grunt, Shotgun Guard, Stalker Rusher, Aegis Warden, and Marksman Sniper.
- Implement an efficient 40px tile-grid A* pathfinder (`GridPathfinder`) for navigating arena geometry with entity radius clearance buffers.
- Implement line-of-sight direct vector steering ("string pulling") to provide natural, fluid pursuit and kiting when sightlines are unobstructed.
- Implement discrete hit-count shields on combat units with procedural audio pings and visual radiant barrier rings.
- Implement charging sightline laser telegraphs for snipers and archetype-specific stutter-step vs run-and-gun firing behaviors.
- Expand and rebalance the puzzle progression from 3 to 5 distinct rooms.

**Non-Goals:**
- Shield recharge timers or regeneration during combat (shields are strictly hit-count based).
- Contact/melee damage (all combat remains lethal ballistic projectile based).
- External audio asset loading (all new sound effects remain 100% procedural Web Audio).

## Decisions

### 1. 40px Grid A* Pathfinding with Line-of-Sight Direct Override

**Choice**: Use a $24 \times 16$ tile grid ($40\text{px}$ cells matching the arena's visual floor grid) with obstacle bounding-box inflation by enemy radius ($16\text{px}$). When line-of-sight to the player is obstructed by obstacles, enemies follow waypoints generated by A*. The moment line-of-sight becomes clear, the AI overrides waypoint following and steers directly along the sightline vector (closing distance for rushers, backing away for kiters).

- **Rationale**: The entire arena is only 384 grid cells. An A* search runs in $<0.05\text{ms}$ in JavaScript, guaranteeing zero pathing overhead and absolute reliability around corners without corner snagging. Switching to direct vector steering in open sightlines avoids robotic 90°/45° stair-stepping.
- **Alternatives Considered**:
  - *Continuous Steering / Raycast feelers*: Highly organic, but prone to getting stuck in U-shaped concave obstacles and corners.
  - *Polygonal Navigation Mesh*: Overkill for a 2D rectangular arena with axis-aligned obstacle blocks and circular pillars.

### 2. Hit-Count Shield Durability and Damage Contract

**Choice**: Extend `CombatUnit` and `Enemy` with `maxShields: number`, `shields: number`, and `takeDamage(damage = 1): { absorbed: boolean; eliminated: boolean }`.
- When hit: If `shields > 0`, decrement `shields`, return `{ absorbed: true, eliminated: false }`, emit radiant barrier spark particles, and trigger `playShieldDeflect()`. If `shields === 0` on the hit that pops it, trigger `playShieldBreak()`.
- When hit with 0 shields remaining: Trigger `kill()`, return `{ absorbed: false, eliminated: true }`, emit crystalline unit shatter particles, and trigger `playShatter()`.

- **Rationale**: Keeps combat deterministic and puzzle-like. Hit-count mechanics interface directly with the player's 6-round revolver cylinder economy without floating-point health bars or ambiguous damage scaling.
- **Alternatives Considered**: Continuous health point pools (introduces DPS math that dilutes 1-hit precision puzzle play).

### 3. Firing Style Coordination: Stutter-Step vs Run-and-Gun

**Choice**: Archetype-specific discharge behavior:
- **Stalker**: Run-and-gun. Maintains full sprint velocity while discharging.
- **Marksman**: Halts completely during a 30-tick telegraph laser charge, firing at tick 30, with a cooldown of 110 ticks.
- **Grunt, Shotgun Guard, Aegis Warden**: Stutter-step. Halts movement for 6–8 ticks upon discharging to telegraph recoil and ballistic commitment.

- **Rationale**: Stutter steps provide critical telegraphing in a top-down tactical shooter, giving the player legible visual cues to dodge incoming bullets. High-speed enemies like Stalkers remain terrifying because they do not stop.

### 4. Procedural Audio Synthesis Extensions

**Choice**: Add three procedural sound generators to `SoundSynthesizer`:
- `playShieldDeflect(timeScale)`: High-frequency dual sine pings (1800Hz / 2800Hz) with rapid exponential decay (0.04s) and pitch scaled by `timeScale`.
- `playShieldBreak(timeScale)`: Resonant low-pass noise burst combined with a descending pitch dispersion wave.
- `playSniperCharge(timeScale)`: Ascending frequency sine sweep with subtle vibrato indicating active laser aim lock.

- **Rationale**: Complies with the zero-asset Web Audio architecture and dynamic time-dilation pitch scaling.

### 5. 5-Room Progression Design

```text
Room 1: Basic Cover (1x Mobile Pistol Grunt)
Room 2: Armored Breach (1x Shotgun Guard [1 Shield] + 1x Grunt)
Room 3: Infiltration (1x Stalker Rusher + 1x Grunt in a corridor layout)
Room 4: The Line of Fire (1x Marksman Sniper + 1x Shotgun Guard with cover bunkers)
Room 5: Tactical Gauntlet (1x Aegis Warden [2 Shields] + 1x Stalker + 1x Grunt)
```

- **Rationale**: Smooth learning curve teaching each archetype in isolation before combining them in Room 5, which tests complete cylinder budgeting (3 bullets for Warden, 1 for Stalker, 1 for Grunt = 5/6 bullets used).

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **[Risk] High CPU load from multiple enemies running A* pathfinding**  
  → **Mitigation**: A* grid is only 384 nodes. Repath queries are cached and throttled to every 15–20 simulation ticks, and bypassed entirely when enemies have direct line-of-sight.

- **[Risk] Enemies clipping or snagging on obstacle corners during movement**  
  → **Mitigation**: The A* grid marks cells within entity radius of obstacles as impassable, and `Enemy.update` incorporates circle-AABB sliding collision resolution matching `Player.ts`.

- **[Risk] Difficulty spike in Room 5 due to 6-round cylinder limit**  
  → **Mitigation**: Level 5 layout provides two robust central pillars where the player can break line-of-sight to safely perform the +30 tick reload cycle.
